FAQ

  • Where is my uploaded mahjong card stored?

    Your card stays on your device. Trinket does not upload it to a Housecat server.

  • Can Housecat see my card?

    No. Your uploaded card is processed and stored locally on your device.

  • Can I replace my card?

    Yes. If you need to upload a better photo or replace a panel, you can re-upload it in Trinket.

  • How do I delete my Trinket data?

    Trinket stores your card and progress locally on your device. Deleting Trinket from your iPhone removes that locally stored data.

  • Something weird happened. What should I send you?

    Tell me what you were doing when it happened and what you expected to happen. A screenshot is always helpful too.
